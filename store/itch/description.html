Pixel Paint turns pixel art into a calm color-by-number puzzle. Every cell of a gray picture shows a number, and the color guide tells you which color it is. Paint each cell with its color and watch the picture come to life.

Everything is cute pixel art: pick a picture from a storybook under a pastel sky, and a little slime friend cheers your combos (and politely hops out of your way).

Play it right here in your browser, or download the Android app.

For kids and grown-ups

29 pictures in four levels. Easy pictures use up to 5 colors, big and clear for small fingers. Expert scenes have up to 40 colors with lots of similar shades: a sleepy cat, a shiba in a meadow, a moon witch, a mermaid reef and a treehouse tea party.

Score and combos

  • Every correct cell scores: 10 points with the Pencil, 3 a cell with Fill
  • Keep painting quickly to build a combo, all the way up to 20x
  • Slow down and the combo slips back one level at a time; a wrong color ends it
  • Need a break? Pause anytime and the combo waits for you
  • Beat your best score on every picture (saved in your browser)
  • The Android app adds an online leaderboard for every picture (Google Play Games)

Prefer it calm? Ignore the combo and paint at your own pace.

Features

  • Pencil: paint cell by cell, or drag across a row
  • Fill: one tap paints a whole touching area of the same number
  • Cells with the selected number light up
  • Zoom into big pictures; a mini map shows where you are
  • Find a cell jumps to the next cell still waiting for your color
  • A wrong color stays marked until you fix it
  • Cozy background music (with an off switch)
  • No ads, no account. Progress is saved on your device.

Controls

  • Touch: tap or drag to paint, pinch to zoom and move
  • Mouse: click or drag to paint, scroll to zoom, right-drag to move

Music starts after your first click (browsers don't allow sound before that). Use the fullscreen button for the most room to paint.
