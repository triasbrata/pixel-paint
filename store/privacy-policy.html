Pixel Paint – Privacy Policy

Effective October 8, 2026

Pixel Paint (“the app”) is a color-by-number game made by {{DEVELOPER}}. This policy covers the Android app and the web version.

In short: the game itself does not collect, send or share any personal information. The only exception is optional: if you sign in to Google Play Games, the scores of finished pictures go to its leaderboards.

What the app stores, and where

The app saves these things so you can pick up where you left off:

  • your painting progress and score for each picture,
  • your best score for each picture, and
  • whether the background music is on or off.

They are kept only on your device (in the app's private storage on Android, or in your browser's local storage on the web). They are never sent anywhere. “Start over” clears a picture's progress; uninstalling the app, or clearing the site's data in your browser, removes everything.

Google Play Games leaderboards (Android, optional)

Each picture has an online leaderboard on Google Play Games. You never have to sign in to play: everything works without it except the leaderboards. If you are signed in to Google Play Games, the score of each finished picture is sent to that picture's leaderboard, along with your Play Games profile (gamer name and avatar), which other players can see there. Like any game using it, the Google Play Games component also collects its own usage and diagnostics data. A score finished while you are signed out is kept on your device until you sign in. Google Play Games is run by Google under the Google Privacy Policy; we don't receive any other information about you through it. The web version has no online leaderboards.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts of its own; signing in to Google Play Games is optional.
  • No advertising and no advertising ID.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking of our own.
  • No in-app purchases.
  • No access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

The Android app does not request permission to use the internet; Google Play Games connects through Google Play services on your device.

Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your country). Google Play Games is optional and never needed to play; for children's accounts it follows the settings their parents choose in Google Family Link.

Stores and hosting

The app is distributed through Google Play and, for the web and Android versions, itch.io. Those services handle downloads and their own accounts under their own privacy policies: Google and itch.io. The game itself sends them nothing.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
