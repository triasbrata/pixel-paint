Pixel Paint turns pixel art into a color-by-number puzzle. Every cell of a gray picture shows a number, and the color guide tells you which color it is. Paint each cell with its color and watch the picture come to life.

Everything is cute pixel art: pick a picture from a storybook under a pastel sky, and a little slime friend cheers your combos (and politely hops out of your way).

29 PICTURES, EASY TO EXPERT
Easy pictures use up to 5 colors, big and clear. Expert scenes have up to 40 colors with lots of similar shades: a sleepy cat, a shiba in a meadow, a moon witch, a mermaid reef and a treehouse tea party.

SCORES, COMBOS AND LEADERBOARDS
• Every correct cell scores: 10 points with the Pencil, 3 a cell with Fill
• Keep painting quickly to build a combo, all the way up to 20x
• Slow down and the combo slips back one level at a time; a wrong color ends it
• Need a break? Pause anytime and the combo waits for you
• Beat your best score on every picture
• Compare scores on a leaderboard for each picture with Google Play Games (optional)

PAINT YOUR WAY
• Pencil: tap or drag across cells to paint them
• Fill: one tap paints a whole touching area of the same number
• Cells with the selected number light up, so they're easy to spot
• Pinch to zoom into big pictures; the mini map shows where you are
• "Find a cell" jumps to the next cell still waiting for your color
• Prefer it calm? Ignore the combo and paint at your own pace

NO ADS
No ads and no in-app purchases. Your progress is saved on your device and the game plays offline; only the leaderboards need a connection and a Google Play Games sign-in. Cozy background music, which you can turn off.

Pictures and UI art from the Cute Fantasy packs by Kenmi; the slime from the Tiny RPG pack by Zerie.
